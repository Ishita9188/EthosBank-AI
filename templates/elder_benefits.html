Senior Citizen Savings Scheme

Government-Backed Savings

A savings scheme designed specifically for senior citizens looking for a relatively stable source of income.

Who may be eligible?

  • Senior citizens meeting the applicable age criteria.
  • Eligible retirees subject to scheme rules.
  • Investment must follow the applicable scheme limits.

How to obtain it

  1. Visit the bank branch or permitted online channel.
  2. Request an SCSS application form.
  3. Provide the required identity and address documents.
  4. Submit the investment amount.
  5. Receive confirmation of the account.
📄 Commonly required documents

Identity proof, address proof, age proof, photographs and applicable account/bank details.
